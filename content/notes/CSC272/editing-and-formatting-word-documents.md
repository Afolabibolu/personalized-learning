# Editing and Formatting Word Documents

## Saving

Save a document with **File, Save** (or Save As, then Browse) or the **Save** button on the Quick Access Toolbar. Closing a document with unsaved changes prompts you to save them. When you reopen a document, Word offers to **pick up where you left off**.

## Formatting text

Select text, then use the **Mini Toolbar** or the **Font group**: **bold, italic and underline**; **strikethrough**; **subscript** (as in H₂O) and **superscript** (as in 21st); **Change Case** (for text typed in the wrong case, such as ALL CAPS); font colour and type; and **Clear Formatting**. **Ctrl + A** selects the whole document.

## Paragraphs

The **default text alignment is left**. The Paragraph group offers left, centre, right and **justify**, which aligns text evenly at **both the left and right** sides. **Line spacing** can be set from **1.0 to 3.0**, and **paragraph spacing** before or after a paragraph is set from the Page Layout tab. **Bullets and numbered lists** organise points, and the **Sort** command sorts a list **ascending or descending** by text, number or date; tick **Header Row** if the list has a heading that should not be sorted.

## Moving and copying

**Cut, Copy and Paste** are in the Clipboard group. **Drag and drop** moves selected text; holding **Ctrl** while dragging **copies** it instead. Text can also be copied between applications.

## Find and replace

**Find** searches for text, formats such as bold, special characters and elements such as footnotes and graphics. **Replace** takes a "Find what" and a "Replace with" entry; **Find Next** skips an entry you do not want to change. **Go To** jumps to a page or other item.

## Page layout and proofing

The **Page Layout** tab sets **orientation** (portrait or landscape), **margins**, vertical alignment and **breaks**. The **Review** tab gives a **word count** (which also counts characters, paragraphs and lines), the **Thesaurus** for synonyms, and **Spelling and Grammar** checking.

## Indents

Indents move paragraphs away from the margin: indent a whole paragraph **from the left**, from **both sides**, only the **first line**, or use a **hanging indent**, where the **second and later lines** are indented.

## Tables

The Insert tab creates a **table**. Cells can be **merged** into one or **split** again, text **centred** in cells, **borders and gridlines** displayed, cells **shaded**, and a **table design** applied from the Table Tools contextual tab. Word's References and Insert tabs can also add **captions**, a **list of figures or tables**, **headers and footers** and **page numbers**, and **section breaks** let different parts of a document have different formats and numbering.
