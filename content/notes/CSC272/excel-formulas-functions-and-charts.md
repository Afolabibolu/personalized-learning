# Excel Formulas, Functions and Charts

## Copying and moving data

**Copy and Paste** (Ctrl + C, then Paste) duplicates data via the **clipboard**: a **flashing border** surrounds the copied cells until you press **Esc** or start another task. **Cut and Paste** moves data within a sheet, to another sheet or to another workbook.

## Formulas

**Formulas** perform calculations by linking cells together. Excel's **default calculation method is automatic**: when a value changes, every formula that refers to it **recalculates**. The four basic operators are **+** (addition), **-** (subtraction), **\*** (multiplication) and **/** (division). **Every formula starts with an equals sign (=)**, which tells Excel that what follows is a formula. To build one, select the answer cell, type =, click the first cell, type an operator, click the next cell, and press Enter. The **result** shows in the cell while the **formula** shows in the **formula bar**. Brackets control the order of calculation.

## Functions

A **function** is a **predefined formula** that operates on values and returns a value; many are shorthand for common formulas:

- Formula: `=A1+A2+A3+A4+A5+A6+A7+A8`
- Function: `=SUM(A1:A8)`

Every function has three elements: it **starts with =**, has a **name**, and has **arguments in brackets**. A few functions, such as **NOW()** (the current date and time), take no arguments.

**AutoSum**, on the Home tab's Editing group and on the Formulas tab, inserts **=SUM** and guesses the range; correct it with the mouse if needed, using **Ctrl** to select non-adjacent cells. The arrow beside AutoSum gives **Average, Count, Max and Min**. The **Formulas tab** holds the full **function library**, and the **fx (Insert Function)** button lets you type a description of what you need and choose from a list.

## Charts

Charts make data **clearer, more interesting and easier to read** and help comparisons. Excel offers **pie, line, bar and column** charts and more. To create one, **select the data** (Ctrl for non-adjacent cells), go to the **Insert tab** and choose a chart type from the Chart group, or use **Recommended Charts** (new in Excel 2013). Selecting a chart shows the **Chart Tools** tabs, **Design and Format**, with tools for chart layout (title and legend), chart styles, chart data (change the range or switch rows and columns), **Change Chart Type** and **Chart Location** (move the chart to its own sheet). **Changes to the source data are reflected in the chart automatically**. Drag a corner handle with **Shift** held to resize it in proportion.

## Printing

**Preview before printing**: **File, Print** shows the sheet exactly as it will print, with settings for the printer, **orientation**, paper size, **margins** and **scaling** to fit a sheet on one page. **Print Selection** prints only a selected range, and a defined **print area** limits printing. **Page Setup** (in Print Preview or on the Page Layout tab) sets orientation and scaling (Adjust to or Fit to), margins and **Centre on Page**, and headers and footers.
