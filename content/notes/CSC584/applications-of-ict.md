# Applications of ICT

## Conferencing and tele-presence

- **Teleconferencing:** a meeting between two or more locations through a telecommunications system, also called phone or audio conferencing. It can carry audio only or audio and video, over telephone, computer, TV, radio and other channels. Internet teleconferencing includes video, web, internet-telephone and augmented reality (AR) conferencing. It is interactive group communication between three or more people in two or more places, carried over traditional PBX systems or VoIP.
- **Video conferencing:** two or more locations communicate by simultaneous **two-way video and audio**. It is also called visual collaboration, a type of groupware. It offers VoIP and can share files, applications and whiteboards.
- **Tele-presence:** technologies that make a person feel, or appear, present at a place other than their true location, sometimes through telerobotics. It is like video conferencing but more advanced, adding:
  - **telecommunication and networking:** terminals, links and nodes with unique addresses (the address space);
  - **tele-computing:** someone controls your system remotely, for example to install or fix software;
  - **messaging:** instant messages ("chatting") with text, pictures or video;
  - **information search, retrieval and archival:** storage in databases or on the Web instead of filing cabinets.

The key difference is that tele-presence adds these technological features to video conferencing.

## ICT-based gadgets

Gadgets are tools that help do something. ICT gadgets include:
- **radio**, for audio;
- **television**, for audio-visual information;
- **computer**, an electronic data processing machine;
- **satellites**, for transmission by cable or wireless;
- **VSAT** (very small aperture terminal), for digital broadcast;
- **fax machine**;
- **telephone**;
- **GSM** (global system for mobile communication), which allows roaming;
- **ATMs**, for balance enquiry, transfers, deposits and withdrawals;
- **point-of-sale** automated cash registers.

## Impacts of ICT on our lives

1. **Education:** computers, tablets and interactive boards replace chalk and heavy books, giving a better learning environment. UNESCO pursues ICT-enhanced education.
2. **Industry:** machines linked to the web raise productivity and cut production time.
3. **Politics and the military:** information is power; radars, drones and encrypted information underpin military strength.
4. **Entertainment:** quick access to films and music, interactive TV, and image making with digital cameras, printers and scanners.
5. **Medical science:** devices and health informatics tools for acquiring, storing and using health information, and for detection and treatment.
6. **Finance:** trading, reporting earnings, budgeting, electronic transfers, credit cards and e-commerce.
7. **Public sector management:** convenient, transparent, accountable and cost-effective government, such as paying bills or renewing licences online.
8. **Home electronics:** systems that control lighting, security and air conditioning.
9. **Agriculture:** better farming techniques, access to resources, production strategies and digital marketing for agribusiness.
10. **Business:** transactions and agreements completed quickly from anywhere in the world.

Since the industrial revolution, people have built machines to ease their work. With information technology, paper-based office work that took a long time is now done quickly, which is why ICT has such a large impact on daily life.
