# The Meaning, Functions and Roles of Management

## Definitions

- "The art of getting things done through and with people in formally organized groups" (Haimann).
- "The process of decision-making and control over the actions of human beings for the express purpose of attaining predetermined goals" (Vance).
- "The coordination of all resources through the process of planning, organizing, directing and controlling in order to attain a given stated objective" (Fayol; Koontz and O'Donnell).

Management is both a social and a technical process.

**Characteristics:** management is an activity; it is purposeful and goal-oriented; it is a social process; it gets things done through people; it is an intangible force; it is an integrating process; it is separate from ownership in modern corporations; it is universal; and it is a social science.

Management is both a **science** (organized knowledge from scientific findings) and an **art** (the know-how to achieve results). The two are complementary: managers without science can only trust luck and intuition.

**Why an IT professional needs management:** it improves efficiency, it shows how to analyse the managerial job, and it helps achieve social goals and responsibilities to shareholders, employees, customers and the state. Success comes from doing the right things (**effectiveness**) and concentrating resources on them (**efficiency**).

## Functions of management

- **Planning:** setting goals and how to achieve them.
- **Organizing:** establishing a structure of working relationships and departments.
- **Staffing:** filling positions with the right people in the right job at the right time.
- **Directing:** leading and motivating people towards the goals.
- **Controlling:** evaluating performance and maintaining and improving it.

## Mintzberg's managerial roles

- **Interpersonal:**
  - **figurehead** (symbol of the organization);
  - **leader** (inspires, trains and coaches);
  - **liaison** (links the organization with outsiders and departments).
- **Informational:**
  - **monitor** (analyses information);
  - **disseminator** (passes it to members);
  - **spokesperson** (promotes the organization).
- **Decisional:**
  - **entrepreneur** (starts projects);
  - **disturbance handler** (handles crises);
  - **resource allocator**;
  - **negotiator** (works out agreements).

## Managerial skills

1. **Technical skills:** specialized knowledge and methods, most needed at supervisory (lower) level.
2. **Human skills:** working with and through people, needed at every level and especially middle management.
3. **Conceptual skills:** seeing the "big picture", diagnosing situations and distinguishing cause from effect, most needed at top level.

## The management environment

- The **internal environment**: mission, goals, management and resources.
- The **market (task) environment**: consumers, suppliers, intermediaries and competitors.
- The **macro environment**: technological, economic, political, legal and social forces.

Organizations must adapt to their environment, which brings opportunities and threats.
