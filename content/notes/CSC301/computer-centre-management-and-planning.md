# Computer Centre Management and Planning

## What computer centre management covers

**Computer centre management** is the planning, organization and coordination of the resources and activities of a computer centre or data centre so that it provides efficient and effective computing services. It is crucial for the **reliability, security and performance** of the IT infrastructure. Its key aspects are:

1. **Infrastructure planning:** procure the hardware (servers, storage, networking equipment) and software the organization needs, and do **capacity planning** for growing workloads.
2. **Facility management:** physical security (access control, surveillance, environmental controls for temperature and humidity), and stable **power and cooling**.
3. **Network management:** design and configure the network for fast, reliable connectivity, and monitor its performance.
4. **System administration:** manage servers, administer user accounts and access permissions, and run **backup and recovery** with disaster recovery plans.
5. **Security management:** protect systems and data, audit for vulnerabilities and comply with standards and regulations.
6. **Resource allocation and utilization:** schedule tasks and workloads, and monitor performance to find bottlenecks.
7. **Service desk and user support:** a **help desk** as the central point of contact, and troubleshooting to minimize downtime.
8. **Budgeting and financial management:** estimate costs for hardware, software and maintenance, and plan finances that align with organizational goals.

It needs technical expertise, strategic planning and operational efficiency.

## Planning in computer centre management

Planning defines goals, determines strategies and outlines the steps to run the centre well and in line with the organization's goals. It covers ten areas:

1. **Strategic planning:** define objectives (reliability, security, growth) and align them with organizational goals.
2. **Infrastructure planning:** assess what exists, and plan capacity for future needs.
3. **Security planning:** assess physical and cyber risks, and set security policies.
4. **Disaster recovery planning:** identify critical systems, and create backup, recovery and continuity plans.
5. **Budgeting and financial planning:** estimate costs and allocate budgets by priority.
6. **Resource allocation planning:** schedule maintenance and updates to minimize disruption, and optimize use of resources.
7. **Training and skill development:** assess the skills needed and run training programmes.
8. **Compliance planning:** keep up with regulations and plan compliance measures.
9. **Communication planning:** communicate with management, staff and users, and plan for emergencies.
10. **Regular review and revision:** assess plans periodically and gather feedback.

Planning is an ongoing process that needs collaboration, flexibility and continuous improvement.
