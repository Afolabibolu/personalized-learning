# Simple Harmonic Motion, Elasticity and Liquids

## Simple harmonic motion

A body displaced from equilibrium feels a **restoring force proportional to its displacement**, so **a = −ω²x**. The solution is **x = A sin(ωt + φ)**, with period T = 2π/ω.

- The velocity is v = ω√(A² − x²): greatest at the centre, zero at the extremes.
- The acceleration is greatest at the extremes.

Two standard oscillators:

- **mass on a spring**: **T = 2π√(m/k)**;
- **simple pendulum** (small angles): **T = 2π√(l/g)**, independent of the mass, as dimensional analysis showed.

A pendulum of length 0.8 m has T = 1.795 s. Swinging as a conical pendulum at 30°, T = 2π√(l cos 30°/g) = 1.670 s, so the error is about **0.125 s**.

## Elasticity

**Hooke's law**: within the elastic limit, **F = k ΔL**.

- **Tensile stress** = F/A and **tensile strain** = ΔL/L₀.
- **Young's modulus Y = stress/strain = FL₀/(AΔL)**. It depends on the material, not on the specimen's size.

**Example**: 98 N on a steel rod of radius 1 mm (Y = 200 GPa) gives stress 3.12 × 10⁷ Pa, strain 1.56 × 10⁻⁴, and an extension of **0.312 mm** in 2 m.

The **stress–strain curve** is linear up to the **elastic limit**. Beyond it come the **yield point** (plastic flow), the **ultimate strength** and the **breaking point**. The stored elastic energy is **½kx² = ½ × stress × strain × volume**.

The other moduli:

- **Bulk modulus K = −V dP/dV** (volume change). For a gas at constant temperature, K = P. The speed of sound is √(K/ρ).
- **Modulus of rigidity η = (F/A)/(x/L)** (shear, a change of shape without a change of volume).

## Liquids

From general knowledge:

- **Pressure** in a liquid is p = hρg, acting equally in all directions.
- **Archimedes' principle**: the **upthrust** equals the weight of fluid displaced.
- **Surface tension** is the force per unit length in a liquid's surface, which makes small drops spherical.
- **Viscosity** is internal friction. In Stokes' law, F = 6πηrv, and a falling sphere reaches a **terminal velocity**. The coefficient of viscosity has dimension **ML⁻¹T⁻¹**.
