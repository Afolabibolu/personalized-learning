# Vectors

## Scalars and vectors

A **scalar** is described by its magnitude alone (mass, time, energy), and scalars add arithmetically: 20 kg + 40 kg = 60 kg. A **vector** needs magnitude **and** direction (displacement, velocity, acceleration, force, electric field). A displacement of 1,000 km from Ibadan means little until the direction is given.

Compatible pairs match type: velocity and **acceleration** and displacement are all vectors, while speed and distance are scalars. So "acceleration & displacement" is a compatible pair.

## Adding vectors geometrically

Place the vectors head to tail. With angle θ between A and B, the **cosine rule** gives the resultant

**R² = A² + B² + 2AB cos θ**,

and the **sine rule** gives its direction. Two pulls of 10 N and 15 N at 60° give **R = 21.8 N at 36.6°** to the 10 N force. For two equal forces P at angle 2θ, R = **2P cos θ**.

**Subtraction**: A − B = A + (−B), so R² = A² + B² − 2AB cos θ.

## Components

Resolve each vector into components: V = Vₓi + Vᵧj + V_zk. Then

- **|V| = √(Vₓ² + Vᵧ² + V_z²)**;
- its direction to the x-axis is θ = tan⁻¹(Vᵧ/Vₓ).

Add vectors by adding components. For D₁ = i + 3j, D₂ = 2i + 3j and D₃ = −i + j, the resultant is 2i + 7j, with magnitude **7.3 m**. (The paper's nearest option is 7.2 m.)

For A = 3i − 2j and B = −i − 4j, A − B = 4i + 2j, so |A − B| = √20 = **4.47**. Two birds flying with velocities i + 4j + 3k and 4i + 2j − 4k have relative velocity (3, −2, −7), so after 5 s they are 5√62 ≈ **39.4 m** apart.

Bearings must be converted carefully: **60° NE** (60° from north towards east) is the same direction as **30° EN**.

## Products

- **Dot product**: A·B = AB cos θ = AₓBₓ + AᵧBᵧ + A_zB_z, a scalar. It is zero for perpendicular vectors.
- **Cross product**: |A × B| = AB sin θ, a vector perpendicular to both. It equals the **area of the parallelogram** on A and B. For (6, 3, 1) and (−2, 4, 5), the area is |(11, −32, 30)| ≈ **45.2 m²**.
