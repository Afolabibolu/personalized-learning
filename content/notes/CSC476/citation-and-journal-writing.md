# Citation, Referencing and Journal Writing

## Citation and citation styles

A **citation** gives credit to the people whose creative and intellectual work supports our research and lets readers locate the source. A **citation style** dictates what information is given, in what order, and how it is punctuated:

- **APA** (American Psychological Association): education, psychology and the sciences
- **MLA** (Modern Language Association): the humanities
- **Chicago/Turabian:** business, history and the fine arts
- **IEEE:** computing and other sciences

## APA reference list

An APA reference list must be in **alphabetical order** by the first author's surname (or by title if there is no author, ignoring "a", "an" and "the"). Works by the same author in the same year are ordered by title and given letters (2017a, 2017b). It must contain full references for every in-text citation.

## APA in-text citation

In-text citations follow a quote or paraphrase and give only the **author's surname and the year**; a direct quote also needs a page number.

- **Two authors:** join the surnames with "and" in the text or "&" in brackets: Akinola and Usman (2017) assert ...; (Akinola & Usman, 2017).
- **Three to five authors:** name all of them the first time: (Akinola, Usman, & Chukwudi, 2017); later, use the first author and **et al.**: (Akinola et al., 2017).
- **Six or more authors:** use the first author and et al. from the first citation.
- **APA 7th edition (2020):** the course's APA7-Style.pdf guide simplifies this: for **three or more** authors, use the first author and et al. every time.
- **No author:** use a short title in quotation marks: ("APA Citation", 2017).
- **Several sources for one point:** list them in one bracket, in alphabetical order, separated by **semicolons**.

## APA reference formats

- **Book:** Mitchell, J. A., Thomson, M., & Coyne, R. P. (2017). A guide to citation. London, England: Willey.
- **Edited book:** add (Ed.); a **chapter** gives the chapter author, then "In" the editor (Ed.), the book title and the pages.
- **E-book:** replace the publisher with "Retrieved from" and the URL.
- **Journal article:** replace the publisher with the journal title, volume, issue and pages: Mitchell, J.A. (2017). Citation: Why is it so important? Mendeley Journal, 67(2), 81-95.

## IEEE style

IEEE uses **numbers in square brackets**, such as [1]. The reference list is ordered **numerically**, not alphabetically.

## Writing a journal article

Before writing, read widely and identify a **potential journal** whose scope fits the work, checking that it is properly **indexed**, for example in Scopus. Follow the journal's guidelines and write the article in the standard way.

**Citation metrics:**

- The **h-index** is the largest h such that the author has h papers cited at least h times each.
- The **i10-index** is the number of papers with at least ten citations.
