# Digital Business and Contemporary Entrepreneurship

## Contemporary entrepreneurship

**Traditional** entrepreneurship relies on physical assets, local customers and slower growth, measured mainly by profit. **Contemporary** entrepreneurship uses **digital platforms**, **global reach from day one**, faster innovation, **partnerships and ecosystems**, and purpose. "Entrepreneurship is not a solo journey":

- **Networking** is "opportunity currency": personal, academic, professional, digital (LinkedIn) and mentor networks.
- **Mentors** help you avoid mistakes, learn faster and access networks.
- **Role models** show that success is possible.
- **Leadership** needs communication, initiative, reliability, team leadership and a **problem-solving mindset**; a **solo founder** risks burnout, so find partners with complementary skills.

## Digital business and e-commerce

A **digital business** uses **technology, data and the internet** to create value, streamline operations and improve customer experience, integrating technology into **all aspects** of the organisation. It is data-driven and proactive, while traditional business relies on manual processes and reactive decisions. Data helps forecast demand, optimise operations and reduce risk, and work becomes remote and global.

**E-commerce** is specifically the **buying and selling of goods and services online**: product search, ordering and inventory, **digital payments** and delivery. Its models are:

- **B2C** (business-to-consumer): Amazon, Jumia, Konga.
- **B2B** (business-to-business): Alibaba.
- **C2C** (consumer-to-consumer): eBay, Facebook Marketplace.
- **C2B** (consumer-to-business): freelancers on Upwork, influencer marketing.

E-commerce focuses on **transactional** value, while digital business focuses on **holistic** value: "digital business transforms **how** a business operates, not just **where** it sells." Examples are **Netflix** (data-driven personalisation), **Flutterwave**, **Paystack** and **Moniepoint** (payments), **Uber**, **Bolt** and **Airbnb**, and **Nike**'s fitness apps.

## Digital marketing

**Digital marketing** covers all online marketing through digital channels, with **precise targeting** and real-time tracking; it offers high reach, low cost and measurable results. Its channels are:

- **SEO**, which ranks content higher in search results;
- **social media marketing**, which builds "social proof";
- **content marketing** and **email marketing**;
- **pay-per-click (PPC)** advertising, paid only when users click;
- **affiliate marketing**, where marketers earn commission through unique links; and
- **analytics**, such as Google Analytics.

A **social media strategy** begins by **setting clear goals**, **researching the audience** and **choosing platforms**: LinkedIn for B2B, Instagram for visuals, TikTok for trends.

## Online branding

An **online brand** is the **perception people form** of a business from how it appears and communicates digitally: its user experience, **visual identity** and messaging. **Visibility + credibility = competitive advantage.** In colour psychology, **blue** signals trust and security, **yellow** creativity and **black** luxury.
