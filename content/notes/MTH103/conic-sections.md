# Conic Sections

## Eccentricity

A conic is the locus of a point P for which **PS = e·PM**, where S is the focus, PM is the distance to the directrix and e is the **eccentricity**.

- **e = 1**: parabola.
- **e < 1**: ellipse.
- **e > 1**: hyperbola.

## Parabola

With focus (a, 0) and directrix x = −a, PS = PM gives **y² = 4ax**. Its slope is dy/dx = 2a/y, so the **tangent** at (x₁, y₁) is **yy₁ = 2a(x + x₁)**, and the normal has slope −y₁/2a. For y² = 4px the tangent at (x₀, y₀) is **yy₀ = 2p(x + x₀)**.

A parabola can also be given parametrically. x = 6t, y = 3t² gives x² = 12y, and the tangent there is **tx − y − 3t² = 0**.

## Ellipse

Here PS = ePM with e < 1 gives **x²/a² + y²/b² = 1**, with **b² = a²(1 − e²)** and a > b.

- Vertices: (±a, 0) and (0, ±b).
- **Foci: (±ae, 0)**; **directrices: x = ±a/e**.
- If the major axis is **vertical**, the equation is x²/b² + y²/a² = 1, with foci (0, ±ae).
- If the centre is (x₁, y₁), replace x by x − x₁ and y by y − y₁, and complete the square to find the centre.

**Example**: e = 4/5 and foci (0, ±4) give ae = 4, so a = 5 and b² = 25 − 16 = 9. The ellipse is **x²/9 + y²/25 = 1**.

**Example**: the sum of distances from two points 8 apart is 14, so 2a = 14, ae = 4 and b² = 49 − 16 = 33. The ellipse is **x²/49 + y²/33 = 1**.

The tangent at (x₁, y₁) is **xx₁/a² + yy₁/b² = 1**, with slope −b²x₁/(a²y₁).

## Hyperbola

With e > 1 the equation is **x²/a² − y²/b² = 1**, with **b² = a²(e² − 1)**.

- Vertices: (±a, 0); foci: (±ae, 0); directrices: x = ±a/e.
- The tangent at (x₁, y₁) is **xx₁/a² − yy₁/b² = 1**, with slope b²x₁/(a²y₁).
- The **asymptotes** are **y = ±(b/a)x** (general knowledge). For 9x² − 4y² = 36, that is x²/4 − y²/9 = 1, they are y = ±(3/2)x.
