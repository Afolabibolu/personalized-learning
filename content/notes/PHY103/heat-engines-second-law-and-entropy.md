# Heat Engines, the Second Law and Entropy

## Heat engines

A **heat engine** takes heat Q_H from a hot reservoir, does work W and rejects **Q_C** to a cold reservoir: **W = Q_H − Q_C**. Its **efficiency** is **η = W/Q_H = 1 − Q_C/Q_H**. No engine turns all its heat into work.

**Example**: a 5 kW engine with 25% efficiency rejecting 8000 J per cycle absorbs Q_H = 8000/0.75 = **10,667 J** per cycle and does 2667 J of work, so each cycle lasts about **0.53 s**. (The typed paper's options do not include these exact values.)

## The Carnot engine

A reversible cycle of two isotherms and two adiabats. Its efficiency is

**η_Carnot = 1 − T_C/T_H** (in kelvin),

the **highest possible** between those temperatures.

- Between 500 K and 400 K: η = 20%, so 10 kJ in gives **2 kJ** of work.
- Between 500 K and 300 K: η_max = 40%, so a claim of 750 J or 500 J per kJ is impossible. Only design C (250 J) can work.
- With η = 30% and T_C = 300 K: T_H = 300/0.7 = **429 K**.
- Between 235 °C and 115 °C: η = 120/508 = 23.6%, so 6.3 × 10⁴ J gives about **15 kJ**.

## Refrigerators

A refrigerator uses work W to move heat Q_C from cold to hot. Its **coefficient of performance** is **COP = Q_C/W**, and the ideal value is **T_C/(T_H − T_C)**. Between −20 °C and 40 °C, the ideal COP is 253/60 = **4.2**. A 1 kW unit with COP 2 removes **2 kW** from the freezer, about **48%** of ideal.

## The second law

- **Kelvin–Planck**: no cyclic engine can convert heat entirely into work.
- **Clausius**: heat cannot flow by itself from cold to hot.

All **natural processes are irreversible**, and no real engine is as efficient as a Carnot engine between the same reservoirs.

## Entropy

For a reversible process, **dS = dQ/T**, in J/K. Melting 1 kg of ice at 273 K: ΔS = 3.35 × 10⁵/273 = **1.23 × 10³ J/K**, an increase. Isothermal heat transfer is Q = TΔS: at 405 K with ΔS = 46 J/K, Q = **18.6 kJ**.

The entropy of an isolated system never decreases: dS ≥ dQ/T, with equality only for reversible processes. A reversible adiabatic process is **isentropic**. The **third law**: absolute zero cannot be reached in a finite number of steps.
