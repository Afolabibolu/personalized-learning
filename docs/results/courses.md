# Courses in the knowledge base

CSC 531 and CSC 574 were built by hand and reviewed. Every other course was added through the ingestion pipeline: the 500-level courses, GES 103 and a first CSC 412 in Phase 9; COS 102 on 25 October, when gaps in CSC 554 and CSC 582 were also filled from general knowledge (marked in the table); the 400-level courses CSC 412 (rebuilt), CSC 421, CSC 431, CSC 472, CSC 475 and CSC 476 on 26 October; and the twelve 300-level courses (CSC 301, 302, 313, 321, 322, 331, 334, 335, 341, 351, 381 and MAT 352) on 27 October: `content/ingest/inventory.py`, then `extract.py`, then the course files written from the extracted text and the page images of scanned papers (default mode, no API key), then `validate.py` and `load.py`. These are sample courses (`is_reviewed = false`) and the app labels them "Sample content, pending lecturer review". They have no lecture links, because `content/sources/links.csv` has none for them yet, except the 300- and 400-level courses. Each 300-level course links to its own Drive folder (CSC 381 and MAT 352 to their past-questions folders, since those hold no lectures), and the first-semester ones also to 2024-2025-300-PQ.pdf. The 400-level courses: each of their topics links to the course's own Drive folder (added 27 Oct from the links supplied; the app labels it "Open lecture folder") and to `400 Level PQ.pdf`, the Drive file of the 2022/23 papers. Their getting-started list is therefore topic 1's note, the lecture folder, then topic 2's note.

The app groups courses by level on the Courses page (`courses.level`, from the course code; GES 103 is set to 200 in its course file because it is filed under 200 level). Sample size per course: 4 to 7 topics (CSC 572 and the 400-level courses have 7), a study note of 300 to 500 words per topic, 8 practice questions per topic in at least three formats, and every past question found for the course.

## Videos and textbooks (25 October)

Every topic has a recommended textbook card (`content/textbooks/<code>.json`): the book in the course folder where there is one (Interaction Design for CSC 531, the course books for CSC 412 (Ojo) and CSC 476, Ramakrishnan and Gehrke for CSC 472 (its solutions manual is in the folder), Mishra and Chandrasekaran for CSC 552, Slonneger and Kurtz for CSC 554, Nielsen for CSC 572, Ng for CSC 574, the GES 103 course book, Computer Science Illuminated for COS 102), otherwise a standard textbook for the subject, with the chapters to read. 185 of the 187 topics have a YouTube video (the 42 400-level topics were added on 26 October and the 84 300-level topics on 27 October), chosen from YouTube's own search results (university courses such as MIT OpenCourseWare and NPTEL, and established teaching channels) and confirmed with YouTube's oEmbed endpoint; CSC 584's Design–Reality Gap and CSC 554's Algebraic Semantics have none, because no result clearly matched the topic.
