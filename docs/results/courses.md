# Courses in the knowledge base

CSC 531 and CSC 574 were built by hand and reviewed. Every other course was added through the ingestion pipeline: the 500-level courses, GES 103 and a first CSC 412 in Phase 9; COS 102 on 25 October, when gaps in CSC 554 and CSC 582 were also filled from general knowledge (marked in the table); the 400-level courses CSC 412 (rebuilt), CSC 421, CSC 431, CSC 472, CSC 475 and CSC 476 on 26 October; the twelve 300-level courses (CSC 301, 302, 313, 321, 322, 331, 334, 335, 341, 351, 381 and MAT 352) on 27 October; and, in November, the 31 200-level courses and the 14 100-level courses (COS 102 rebuilt from its own CCMAS lectures, replacing the 25 October version): `content/ingest/inventory.py`, then `extract.py`, then the course files written from the extracted text and the page images of scanned papers (default mode, no API key), then `validate.py` and `load.py`. These are sample courses (`is_reviewed = false`) and the app labels them "Sample content, pending lecturer review". They have no lecture links, because `content/sources/links.csv` has none for them yet, except the 300- and 400-level courses. Each 300-level course links to its own Drive folder (CSC 381 and MAT 352 to their past-questions folders, since those hold no lectures), and the first-semester ones also to 2024-2025-300-PQ.pdf. The 400-level courses: each of their topics links to the course's own Drive folder (added 27 Oct from the links supplied; the app labels it "Open lecture folder") and to `400 Level PQ.pdf`, the Drive file of the 2022/23 papers. Their getting-started list is therefore topic 1's note, the lecture folder, then topic 2's note.

The app groups courses by level (`courses.level`, from the course code; GES 103 is set to 200 in its course file because it is filed under 200 level): the Courses page shows one card per level, and each level page (`/courses/level/<level>`) splits its courses by semester (`courses.semester`, 1 November). Each course file's `semester` comes from its source folder (FIRST SEMESTER, SECOND SEMESTER, 1 semester, 2 semester); the 400-level folder has no semester folders, so those come from the course's 2022/23 paper, which for all six says First Semester. Sample size per course: 4 to 7 topics (CSC 572 and the 400-level courses have 7), a study note of 300 to 500 words per topic, 8 practice questions per topic in at least three formats, and every past question found for the course.
